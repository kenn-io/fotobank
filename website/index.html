04 / Operating principles

The archive stays understandable.

Exact originals

Imports do not mutate the source.

Content identity is computed before Docbank accepts the bytes. Retries either find the same record or stop on a conflict.

Ordinary working files

Tools work through explicit checkouts.

Selected versions become normal files. Settled edits commit as new immutable versions instead of rewriting the archive behind its back.

Rebuildable views

Indexes and previews are not authority.

Thumbnails, extracted metadata, and search projections can be regenerated from a known content version.

Visible boundaries

Privacy and sharing stay in the product layer.

Fotobank checks ownership and visibility before it lists, serves, exports, or shares a record.
